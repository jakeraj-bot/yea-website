After-school program

Homework help, STEAM, arts, and sports for children 2½–13 — meaningful enrichment after the school day ends at our Paterson school sites.

Our after-school program keeps learning fun and engaging long after the school day ends. We partner with schools and families to provide a safe, structured environment where children build skills, friendships, and confidence.
