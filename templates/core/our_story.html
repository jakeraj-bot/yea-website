Our story

Where every child in our community finds the support they need to truly thrive.

Making a "YEA" of a difference

At Youth Education Academy, we inspire curiosity and a love for learning through dynamic after-school and summer programs that spark creativity and critical thinking. We empower youth with the tools, skills, and confidence they need to excel. We help every student set and reach their goals, ensuring they achieve success not only in school, but in life.

It takes a village

We know that raising confident, capable, and resilient children is a team effort. That's why we partner with families, schools, and communities to provide the support, guidance, and opportunities every child deserves. Through academic enrichment, hands-on learning, and mentorship, we become an extension of your village.

Our mission

To support students and families through impactful, district-aligned afterschool programs, tutoring, and enrichment opportunities.

Our vision

To create positive pathways for youth by offering safe, engaging environments where education and experiential learning can empower communities to succeed.

Core values

  • Growth — Personal development, emotional maturity, and social confidence.
  • Curiosity — Sparking questioning minds that stay active in lifelong learning.
  • Resilience — Strength through challenges, learning to adapt and keep moving forward.
  • Excellence — High standards with consistent guidance to help every learner grow.
