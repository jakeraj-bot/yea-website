4Cs families {% include "enrollment/includes/required_mark.html" %}

  • If you have 4Cs, email your contract to ezchildtracking@yeanj.org. The contract will be filled out and sent back to you by email.
  • Before your child can start, they need:
    • Their name in our 4Cs portal
    • A first-week deposit of $70 (refundable once they are in our portal)
  • If they are not in the portal and you do not want to pay the deposit, they can start once they are in the portal.
  • All 4Cs copays are determined by 4Cs, not YEA. Once your contract appears in our 4Cs portal we will notify you of your copay. You are then responsible to pay weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly depending on your payment plan.
