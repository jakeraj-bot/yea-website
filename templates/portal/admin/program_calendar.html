Program calendar

When YEA is open for parents, plus CC week numbers and how many weeks each month bills. This is separate from 4Cs contract dates.

Two calendars. 4Cs still pays from each child’s contract start (for example 9/1/2026) on the agency form. Parents are billed from the program start date below. Example: program starts 9/8/2026 — that short week is CC WK 1 (9/8/2026–9/11/2026). A bi-weekly parent copay covers 9/8/2026–9/18/2026, not 9/1–9/11. 4Cs still includes the week of 9/1.

Program dates

One date per line for days off and half days. Use 9/8/2026 or 2026-09-08. Half days are stored here; they do not change the weekly copay amount. Saving rebuilds CC week numbers and the month map from the start date. The Thursday of each week owns that month. Overridden week numbers, date spans, and month week lists are kept.

Months

The Thursday of each CC week owns the month. Example: week 9/28/2026–10/2/2026 has Thursday 10/1/2026, so it is October, not September. Not every month is 5 weeks — October 2026 is 5 (Thu 10/1, 10/8, 10/15, 10/22, 10/29) and November 2026 is 4 (Thu 11/5, 11/12, 11/19, 11/26). Saving the program start date fills this list. Override the week count or the CC WK numbers if you disagree. Monthly charges use this list: October - Monthly Charge - for CC WK 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

CC week numbers

Charges use these labels automatically: CC WK 1 - Weekly Charge - for (9/8/2026 - 9/11/2026), CC WK 1 - 4Cs Copay Weekly Charge - for (9/8/2026 - 9/11/2026), and October - Monthly Charge - for CC WK 4, 5, 6, 7, 8. Week numbers sit in the Week column, not under each day. A week counts in the month of its Thursday (9/28/2026–10/2/2026 is October because Thursday is 10/1/2026). Open the Months tab to see or override how many weeks each month bills.
