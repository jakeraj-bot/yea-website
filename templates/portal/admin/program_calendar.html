Program calendar

When YEA is open for parents, plus CC week numbers used on charges. This is separate from 4Cs contract dates.

Two calendars. 4Cs still pays from each child’s contract start (for example 9/1/2026) on the agency form. Parents are billed from the program start date below. Example: program starts 9/8/2026 — that short week is CC WK 1 (9/8/2026–9/11/2026). A bi-weekly parent copay covers 9/8/2026–9/18/2026, not 9/1–9/11. 4Cs still includes the week of 9/1.

Program dates

One date per line for days off and half days. Use 9/8/2026 or 2026-09-08. Half days are stored here; they do not change the weekly copay amount. Saving rebuilds CC week numbers from the start date. Overridden week numbers or date spans are kept.

CC week numbers

Charges use these labels automatically: CC WK 1 - weekly charge, CC WK 1 - weekly 4Cs Copay, and October monthly charge for Wk 4, 5, 6, 7, 8. A week counts in the month of its Friday (9/28/2026–10/2/2026 is October).
