Email

How outgoing mail works

  • Portal sending email is the From address for password resets, invoices, late-fee notices, charge emails, reminders, and other automatic mail. Create a new mailbox (not your personal inbox) and type it here when you have it.
  • Send automatic email when a charge is posted is off until you turn it on. Weekly plan posts, 4Cs copay, bulk Post today, and staff charges will not email parents while it is off.
  • When a staff person uses Email parent or Email parents, the parent still replies to that staff person. The message is sent through the portal address so it delivers reliably, and Reply-To is the staff login email (assistant sends → parent replies go to the assistant).
  • Always BCC puts a copy of every parent email in the portal mailbox so you can see what went out without filling your personal inbox. Leave BCC blank to use the same address as Portal sending email.
  • On the compose screens, check extra staff (assistant, business partner) or type an address to CC them. Parents can see who was included.
